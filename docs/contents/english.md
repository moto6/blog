## 2. Fundamental Grammar Rules & Patterns

### A. Question Word Order (Inversion)
In English, statement word order is **[Subject + Verb]**, but questions reverse this to **[Verb + Subject]** (or use an auxiliary verb like *do/is/can*).

* **Statement:** "Party is a neutral word."
* **Question (Original):** "party is .. more some nutral word right?"
* **Corrected Question:** "Is 'party' a more neutral word?"

### B. Parts of Speech: Nouns vs. Verbs vs. Adjectives
Make sure the word matches its role in the sentence:

* **Describe** (Verb) vs. **Description** (Noun)
  * *Original:* "if your descride is right..."
  * *Corrected:* "If your **description** is accurate..."
* **Nutritionally / Neutrally** (Adverb) vs. **Neutral** (Adjective)
  * *Original:* "in point of nutral... give advice neutrally"
  * *Corrected:* "Provide feedback from a **neutral** perspective."

### C. Article Usage (*a*, *an*, *the*)
Countable singular nouns in English almost always need an article before them:

* *Original:* "i wanna know using about in word party"
* *Corrected:* "I want to know how **the** word 'party' is used."

## 3. Natural Phrasing & Sentence Structure

* **In my point** $\rightarrow$ **In my opinion** / **From my perspective**
  * *Original:* "in my point party is more fun..."
  * *Revised:* "In my opinion, 'party' sounds more fun..."

* **In American's sight** $\rightarrow$ **To Americans** / **From an American perspective**
  * *Original:* "in american's sight is realy fun..."
  * *Revised:* "To Americans, is it meant as a fun joke?"

* **Using about in word** $\rightarrow$ **How the word is used**
  * *Original:* "i wanna know using about in word 'party'"
  * *Revised:* "I want to know how the word 'party' is used here."

## 4. Advanced Vocabulary Upgrades

Elevate everyday vocabulary with more precise and formal options:

| Everyday Word | Upgraded Replacement | Context Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I wanna know** | **I am curious about...** / **I would like to inquire...** | "I am curious about how this historical term evolved." |
| **I feel** | **I have the impression that...** / **I perceive...** | "I have the impression that 'party' carries a lighter tone." |
| **Differences** | **Distinction** / **Nuance** | "What is the subtle distinction between 'liberty' and 'freedom'?" |
| **Proper** | **Appropriate** / **Precise** | "Is 'Boston Tea Incident' a more precise translation?" |
| **Big / Heavy event** | **Significant** / **Momentous** | "The Boston Tea Party was a significant event." |
| **Understand** | **Grasp** / **Comprehend** | "I now grasp the historical context." |
| **Show** | **Illustrate** / **Demonstrate** | "This example illustrates the concept well." |
| **Change** | **Shift** / **Transform** | "The term experienced a slight shift in meaning." |
| **Idea** | **Concept** / **Perspective** | "From my perspective, the phrasing makes sense." |
